# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the technical merits of a change.
Disagreement about design decisions is expected and welcome — personal attacks, harassment, or
discriminatory language are not, and will result in removal from the project's spaces (issues,
pull requests, discussions).

## Scope

This applies to all interactions in this repository's issues, pull requests, and any other
official project space.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be made to the maintainers (see the repository's contact
information on GitHub). All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a
response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

This is a short, practical policy for a small project rather than a full adoption of any external
template — the standard is simple: be someone other contributors want to keep working with.
